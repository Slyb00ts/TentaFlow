@@ en
On the Bloch sphere |0⟩ is the north pole, |+⟩ lies on the X axis and `|−i⟩ = (|0⟩ − i|1⟩)/√2` on the negative Y axis.

Take the qubit from |0⟩ to |−i⟩. Hint: H gives |+⟩, and a quarter turn about the Z axis carries it along the equator. Do not measure the qubit.
