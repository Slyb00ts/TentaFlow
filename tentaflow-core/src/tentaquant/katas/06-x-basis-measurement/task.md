Szkielet przygotowuje kubit w stanie `|−⟩ = (|0⟩ − |1⟩)/√2`. Pomiar w bazie Z daje dla niego 50/50, bo faza jest niewidoczna.

@@ en
The skeleton prepares the qubit in `|−⟩ = (|0⟩ − |1⟩)/√2`. A Z-basis measurement gives 50/50 for it, because the phase is invisible.

Add one gate before the measurement so that it tells |+⟩ from |−⟩ and always gives `1`. To pass: TVD from {1: 1.0} below 0.05 and no `0` counts at all.
